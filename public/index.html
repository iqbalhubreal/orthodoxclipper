How to use
Two boxes: Top becomes the top half of the vertical video and Bottom the bottom half. Drag inside a box to move it. Drag any edge or corner dot to change its width and height separately. Use the slider to check other moments; the small preview shows the exact result. Fill crops the box edges to fill its half, Fit shows the whole box with black bars. "Apply to all clips" uses these boxes for every clip.
